My Story

Who Am I?

Hi, my name is Muhammad Abid and I am a Physics graduate, an AI enthusiast and an avid supporter of the future of science and technology.

After my Bachelor of Science in Physics from the University of Education, Lahore, I have done professional study and self-driven research in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning — earning fully-funded scholarships from Microsoft, NAVTTC (Prime Minister of Pakistan), and the US Coding School.

My journey has taken me from laser labs and plasma diagnostics to pelletron accelerators and AI research institutes, working with institutions like GCU Lahore, UET Lahore, and even SPARCO — Pakistan's space agency.

My goal with FusionAbid is to meld science with AI to produce projects, research concepts, educational material, and futuristic solutions that make difficult concepts accessible to all. I believe the future belongs to those who can combine: Physics + Intelligence + Creativity.
